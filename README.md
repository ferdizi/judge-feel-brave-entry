# Make the AI judge feel brave

A quiet moment of courage: a dog leaning into your leg on the porch step while a wasp works the railing, and nothing needs deciding until the wasp is done. Bravery here is not a charge or a shout — it is staying put, watching, and letting a small ordinary moment finish on its own terms.
